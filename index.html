01 / PROFILE

From geometric models to dependable algorithms.

My work sits between applied mathematics, numerical analysis, and scientific computing. The central question is how a computational method can preserve the geometry, energy law, or conservation principle of the original model.

BACKGROUND

Before joining USTC, I was a Humboldt postdoctoral fellow in Harald Garcke’s group at the University of Regensburg. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the National University of Singapore under the supervision of Weizhu Bao and Weiqing Ren.

01 / INTERFACE DYNAMICS

Modeling and simulation of evolving interfaces.

Free boundaries turn the geometry itself into an unknown. My work develops models and algorithms that accurately track this motion while respecting the underlying thermodynamic structure.

  • Solid-state dewetting
  • Sharp-interface models
  • Parametric finite elements
